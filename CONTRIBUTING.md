# Contributing to OpenPrevue

Thank you for your interest in OpenPrevue! We appreciate the community enthusiasm and passion for reviving the authentic 1990s television program guide experience for modern local events and homelab setups.

## Project Ownership and Maintenance Philosophy

OpenPrevue is conceived, architected, and maintained by **upioneer**. 

The project is an opinionated, specialized recreation dedicated to authentic 1990s broadcast television aesthetics (NTSC CRT simulation, RF headend DSP audio filtering, periodic scrolling cadences, and retro typography) combined with modern event discovery and local offline AI capabilities.

To preserve the architectural integrity, visual authenticity, and unified design language of the project, all core design and codebase reservations are held by the project maintainer. Unsolicited direct code pull requests are generally not merged.

## How to Engage and Contribute

While the codebase is closely curated, community feedback, display diagnostics, and feature ideas are warmly welcomed and highly valued. The best ways to contribute are:

* **File Bug Reports:** If you encounter unexpected behavior, browser rendering differences, scanline artifacts, or data ingestion issues, please open a detailed issue on the official GitHub Issues tracker. Include your operating system, browser version, and reproduction steps.
* **Propose Ideas and Features:** If you have an idea for a retro broadcast feature, an additional ticket provider, or an analog CRT enhancement, please open a GitHub Issue tagged as a feature request.
* **Share Display Configurations:** Share photos, setup details, and diagnostics of OpenPrevue running on vintage CRT televisions, arcade cabinets, smart displays, wall mounts, and micro-PCs (such as Raspberry Pi).
* **Community Feedback:** Sharing your experiences helps guide future roadmap priorities and hardware compatibility improvements.

## Guidelines for Opening Issues

When creating a new issue, please provide the following details:

* A clear and descriptive title summarizing the topic.
* Context regarding your environment (for example: Docker, native Python/Node.js, display resolution, orientation).
* Clear steps to reproduce any reported bug, along with relevant log excerpts or screenshots.
* For feature suggestions, an explanation of the use case and how it aligns with authentic 1990s cable TV broadcast presentation.

Thank you again for your support, interest, and engagement with OpenPrevue!
